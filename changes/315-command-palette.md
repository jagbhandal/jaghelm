### Added
- The command palette (Ctrl+K, Cmd+K on macOS, or the header's search
  button) finds what JagHelm watches: the current issues first, worst first,
  then each service, node, UPS, job, backup and integration, with its status,
  where it is and what it is. Choosing one opens its panel over the page you
  are on, as clicking its row does.
- It also finds every Settings section and your Quick Launch links, which
  open where Settings → Layout says, as they do in Quick Launch.
- Its results are grouped under headings, and a middle click or Ctrl-click
  opens one in a new tab.

### Changed
- The command palette has the new design's look.

### Removed
- Themes are no longer in the command palette: switch them with the sun and
  moon button in the header, or in Settings → Appearance.
