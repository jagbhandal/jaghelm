### Added
- A container you removed on purpose no longer lingers as a "Missing" warning for a day: its card now has a Remove button (in the drawer and on the phone) that forgets it at once and clears the warning. It says what it does first, and that a Kuma monitor or `services.yaml` entry that still names it stays until you edit it. If the container comes back, it appears again by itself.

### Fixed
- A confirmation opened from a phone sheet (Remove on a job, for one) could be hidden behind the sheet; it now opens on top of it.
