### Added
- JagHelm now pages a warning when a node has had no container readings from cAdvisor for about five minutes ("Container readings on <node>: no container readings, so container alerts for <node> are blind"), and sends a recovery when the readings return. It replaces the cAdvisor part of Grafana's Monitoring gap rule. A single failed scrape, a Prometheus outage, and nodes cAdvisor never covers (vm-101, MS-S1) do not page.
