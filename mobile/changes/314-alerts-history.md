### Added
- The Alerts tab's History shows every alert change of the last 30 days,
  newest first and grouped by day: what changed (such as "Gitea · Down →
  Up"), where, when, whether it reached your phones, and why. Search the tab,
  or filter it by severity and kind, and both the active issues and History
  narrow to match. A server too old to keep a history says "History needs a
  newer JagHelm server", and the active issues still show.
