### Added
- On a phone, a node's and a container's detail now show the same history
  charts as on a computer: a node's CPU, memory and disk and its
  temperature, a container's CPU and memory, over the last hour, 24 hours
  or 7 days, one chart a row. The range buttons are big enough for a thumb,
  and touching a chart shows every line's value under your finger; slide
  along it to move, lift to hide.
