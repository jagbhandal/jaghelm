### Added
- Node detail and a container's Service detail now show the node's or the
  container's history, as JagHelm on a computer does: CPU, memory, disk and
  temperature for a node, CPU and memory for a container, over the last
  hour, 24 hours or 7 days. Touch a chart to read its values, slide to move
  along it. A JagHelm server too old to have these charts shows none, and
  no error.
