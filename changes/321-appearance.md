### Added
- Settings → Appearance offers an accent: violet (the default), fuchsia or
  cyan. Each is held clear of the status colours and readable in both themes.
- A Density setting, comfortable or compact, applies on the screens already redrawn and is
  saved with the rest of the display settings.

### Changed
- Settings → Appearance is rebuilt in the new look and grouped: Theme, Accent,
  Density, Font family, Branding, Background, Overview sections and Widgets.
  Every setting it had is still there.
- A custom accent colour saved before the accent set is replaced once by the
  nearest of the three, and Appearance says so.
