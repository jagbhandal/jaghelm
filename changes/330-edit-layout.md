### Added
- The Overview has an Edit layout mode: a button in the header opens a panel
  where you can reorder the Overview's sections, show or hide them (the same
  switches as Settings → Appearance → Overview sections), pin services to the
  top of the Services section, and put services in your custom groups. Every
  move has a button as well as dragging, and is announced to screen readers.
  The arrangement is saved with your other settings, so it appears on your other
  devices the next time they load the page, and two devices changing different
  things keep both. Until you change it, the Overview looks exactly as before,
  and Needs attention still shows every failing service whatever you hide.
